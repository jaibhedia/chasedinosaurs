## Recording notes

- **Record VO separately, then cut picture to it.** Narrating while you play gives you bad
  play *and* bad narration.
- **1080p, 60fps for gameplay.** A 30fps capture of a 60fps game looks broken on a big screen.
- **Your energy should drop, not rise, at 0:50.** The chaos section is loud; the AI section
  is you explaining something you're proud of. The contrast is what sells it.
- **Say "Arc", "The Graph", "Privy", "World ID" out loud.** A judge scoring one track needs
  to hear their product named and see it working.
- **Game audio ducks under VO**, comes back up full in the montage gaps.
- **Cut the first frame of dead air.** Judges watch a lot of these back to back.
- If a take of the roadmap section starts sounding like a promise, redo it starting with
  "the plan is" or "next up" — verbal future-tense markers are cheap insurance.
